# Grand Prix Saves

Keeps up to 10 offline Grand Prix saves instead of one.

The Grand Prix screen shows a card for every save, newest first, with a **Create New** card under them. Scroll
through them with Up and Down: the panel on the right shows the selected save's track, round and standings.

- **Accept** on a save continues it.
- **Create New** starts a new Grand Prix in a save of its own. Your other saves stay, and there's no overwrite warning.
- **F2** deletes the selected save, after the game's own confirm.
- Leave a new Grand Prix before it starts and your saves are still there next time.

At 10 saves the Create New card goes; delete one to make room. Offline only.

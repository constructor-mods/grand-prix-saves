Keeps up to **10 offline Grand Prix saves** instead of one. Every save gets its own card on the Grand Prix screen, so you can keep a championship, a one-off weekend and anything else side by side and jump between them.

### Your saves
- One card per save, newest first, with the team you drive for and when you last played it.
- Scroll with Up and Down. The panel on the right shows the selected save's track, round and standings.
- **Accept** continues the save you're on.
- **F2** deletes the selected save, after the game's own confirm. The list updates straight away.

### Create New
- The **Create New** card under your saves starts a new Grand Prix in a save of its own.
- No more "this will overwrite your existing Grand Prix" warning: your other saves stay.
- Change your mind and back out before the race? Your saves are still there next time.
- Up to 10 saves. At 10 the Create New card goes; delete one to make room.

### Needs
- Swofty Launcher 1.8.10 or newer.
- Offline sessions only, like all modded play.
